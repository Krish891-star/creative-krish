Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our AI-enhanced design services.

We use AI tools to analyze current design trends, color psychology, and audience preferences. This data informs our creative process, helping us make design decisions that maximize engagement and effectiveness. The AI doesn't replace our designers but empowers them with insights that lead to better results.

Absolutely! For most projects, we provide 2-3 AI-generated concept options based on your initial brief. These concepts serve as starting points that our designers then refine according to your feedback.

Our designers carefully curate and modify all AI-generated elements to ensure your designs are unique. We never use raw AI output as final designs. Instead, we treat AI as a collaborative tool that our designers then adapt and enhance with custom elements.

We provide all standard file formats based on your project type. For print materials you'll receive PDF, PNG, and source files. For digital banners: PNG, JPG, and WebP. Video projects are delivered in MP4, MOV, and social-media optimized formats.

While we focus primarily on design services, we have partnerships with high-quality printers and can coordinate the printing process for you. For wedding cards, we offer complete design-to-print packages.
